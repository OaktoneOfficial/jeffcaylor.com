About

A life in music. A company for the stage.

I started as an artist. My debut record, Okay, was named one of Christianity Today's top albums of 2007. A second solo record followed. Then my wife, Leora, and I formed the dream pop duo The Weathering and released a full-length album and an EP.

For sixteen years I lived in Hong Kong. I led worship at Island ECC, where we released several projects with the worship team, and I became one of the first Ableton Certified Trainers in Asia. Playback was part of every Sunday. That's where Oaktone began.

Today we build MIDI controllers and playback tools in Franklin, Tennessee, for engineers on tours around the world.
